Read our vision

Our vision

Make knowledge work executable, observable, and learnable.

At the core of Office Intelligence are executable simulated organizations: persistent environments where agents take on roles, use tools, coordinate under rules, and work toward shared goals over time. These organizations form a shared environment layer for dynamic evaluation, synthetic data generation, and reinforcement-learning-based post-training.
