01 Research in progress

From isolated tasks
to continuing work.

We are developing persistent, role-based environments where new assignments arise from ongoing professional activity. Requests lead to working files; review leads to revisions; accepted work becomes context for the next assignment.

The goal is to connect realistic work generation with agent evaluation and learning. Responsibilities, access permissions, shared materials, and unresolved decisions are part of the environment, not just extra text in a prompt.

Generate environments

Build professional settings with roles, working materials, shared services, and explicit boundaries on what each participant can see and do.

Learn from ongoing work

Connect requests, authorized context, work products, and review feedback into resources for supervised learning and on-policy improvement.

Evaluate the delivery

Assess the submitted artifact and observable outcome: accuracy, completeness, usability, source traceability, and issues that remain unresolved.

02 Work & deliverables

Useful work takes many forms.

A proposal can become a financial model, a slide deck, a visual concept, or a working prototype. Our research vision connects these forms of work, including the reasoning, collaboration, and review that make them useful to the next person.

Documents & shared knowledge

Research a question, draft a proposal, compare contract versions, or maintain a team knowledge base. Keep the writing connected to its sources, decisions, and revisions.

Word / DOCX / PDF / Markdown

Spreadsheets & financial models

Reconcile accounts, plan budgets, forecast cash flow, and compare business scenarios. Keep formulas, assumptions, charts, and source records consistent in editable workbooks.

Excel / XLSX / CSV / structured data

Slides & visual storytelling

Build a research talk, pitch deck, lesson, or board briefing. Connect narrative, layout, charts, and supporting evidence, then revise the presentation for its audience.

PowerPoint / PPTX / presentations

Images & visual design

Develop brand assets, illustrations, interface layouts, diagrams, and campaign imagery. Carry a creative brief through exploration, editing, accessibility checks, and production handoff.

Images / layouts / diagrams / editable design assets

Video, audio & motion

Move from scripts and storyboards to edited clips, motion graphics, voiceovers, and sound. Align timelines, captions, transcripts, and review notes across a production.

Video / audio / animation / subtitles / timelines

3D, CAD & technical design

Explore product concepts, spatial layouts, and engineering alternatives. Connect models and drawings to dimensions, simulations, bills of materials, and technical review.

3D scenes / CAD / STEP / BIM / drawings / BOM

Data, reports & decisions

Clean and join data, investigate trends, build dashboards, and assemble evidence-backed reports. Make findings reproducible and uncertainty visible to the people making decisions.

SQL / datasets / dashboards / reports / evidence

Code, apps & automation

Build a prototype, update an application, connect APIs, or automate a recurring workflow. Deliver working changes with tests, reproducible environments, and maintainable documentation.

Code / scripts / tests / configurations

Communication & coordination

Prepare meetings, draft correspondence, coordinate calendars, and turn discussions into plans. Keep owners, dependencies, approvals, and follow-up work connected across a team.

Email / chat / calendars / minutes / project plans

These are directions for our research, not a fixed capability list. The ambition is useful, reviewable work across formats, professions, and the tools people already use.

03 Professional settings

Different domains.
Shared demands on the work.

From design studios and finance teams to engineering, research, and everyday operations, our vision spans the settings where people turn knowledge into action. Each brings its own materials, constraints, handoffs, and standards of quality.

Illustrative work

Finance & accounting

Connect operating records to editable analyses and explain what the evidence can, and cannot, support.

  • Reconcile deposits, clearing records, and vendor credits.
  • Build cash-flow forecasts with explicit scenario assumptions.
  • Prepare review workpapers and identify missing evidence.
Working materials
Spreadsheets, operating records, reference documents, and analysis scripts.
Review focus
Numerical consistency, source traceability, editable outputs, and unresolved exceptions.

Simulation does not authorize real-world financial, legal, clinical, or operational decisions. Professional review and permission boundaries remain part of the work.
